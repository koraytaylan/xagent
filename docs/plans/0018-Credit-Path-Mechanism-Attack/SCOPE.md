# Scope — Plan 0018

> Attack the credit-assignment mechanism through three concurrent workstreams to unlock vision→action steering above chance (≥0.70).

## Why this plan

**Current state:** Plan 0017 falsified the simple fixes (TD trace-decay hardening to 0.99 and urgency-scaling isolation) — both stayed at chance on the steering-alignment gate. The steering probe (`learning_probe_mirrored_steering_is_chance()`, `integration.rs:2848-2929`) trains agents for 120 episodes on alternating left/right food with dense TD strides (`brain_tick_stride=1, vision_stride=1`), then pins movement speed and measures turn-alignment: the baseline was 0.489 (chance band 0.38–0.62). After prototyping trace decay (0.99) and urgency isolation, alignment remained at 0.441, both failures documented in the falsification record ([`0001-CREDIT-PATH-DECISION.md`](../0017-Credit-Path-And-Emergent-Visual-Evolution/0001-CREDIT-PATH-DECISION.md)). The encoder is verified adequate: the default raycast encoder separates food-left/right with cosine-diff ≈0.0036 (18–24× above within-class noise), and the Gabor cortex achieves 0.964 (55×). The credit path is the bottleneck, not the encoder.

1. **Vision→action steering remains at chance despite encoder separability 18–24× (default raycast) or 55× (Gabor cortex).** The steering probe (`learning_probe_mirrored_steering_is_chance()`, `integration.rs:2848-2929`) trains 120 episodes on alternating left/right food with dense TD strides, then pins movement (`movement_speed=0`) and scores turn-alignment: baseline 0.489, chance band [0.38, 0.62]. Plan 0017 prototyped trace decay and urgency isolation; both stayed in chance band (0.441 vs 0.489 baseline). The encoder separates food-left/right (raycast cosine-diff 0.0036, ~18–24× margin; Gabor 0.964, ~55×), proving the encoder works — the credit path is the suspect.

2. **The credit-path structure produces degenerate learning signals during foraging.** The TD(λ) reward is the urgency-amplified homeostatic gradient: `raw_gradient = energy_delta * ENERGY_WEIGHT + integrity_delta * INTEGRITY_WEIGHT` (`brain_passes.wgsl:913-915`), then `raw_gradient_amplified = raw_gradient * (1.0 + urgency)` (`brain_passes.wgsl:934`). The TD error `δ = clamp(reward + TD_DISCOUNT·V(s′) − V(s), [-MAX_TD_ERROR, MAX_TD_ERROR])` (`kernel_tick.wgsl:1222-1224`) is applied via eligibility traces (critic: `brain_passes.wgsl:1247-1248`, actor: `brain_passes.wgsl:1250-1252`). Plan 0017's variance probe (`urgency_isolation_preserves_learning_signal()`, `0001-CREDIT-PATH-DECISION.md`) measured mean|δ| = 8.7×10⁻⁵, std|δ| = 6.5×10⁻⁵ at mid-energy steady-state: the learning signal is non-zero but extremely small, ~100× below what is needed to move the policy on a single episode.

3. **Three independent mechanisms could explain the steering failure: auxiliary loss bypass of slow TD bootstrap, trace horizon mismatch with sensory latency, or gradient-variance collapse during low-signal episodes.** The raw gradient is sampled once per `vision_stride` (default 10) ticks, but eligibility traces decay with combined `TD_DISCOUNT × TD_LAMBDA = 0.97 × 0.9 = 0.873` per tick (`common.wgsl:560`, `common.wgsl:564`), giving (0.873)^10 ≈ 6.7% retention per vision-frame cycle — traces may decay before credit from that vision frame arrives. Alternatively, an auxiliary objective directly predicting or encoding vision-to-action alignment could bypass the slow bootstrap. Or the credit signal itself could be non-degenerate but shaped poorly for policy learning (normalization, TD-error scaling, or auxiliary supervision could help).

4. **Plan 0017 proved encoder adequacy and rejected simple trace/urgency tweaks; this plan attacks the credit mechanism itself through three independent hypotheses, each measurement-gated on steering alignment ≥0.70.** Workstream 0001 designs an auxiliary steering objective — a self-supervised loss contrasting or predicting the policy's turning direction from encoded vision, supervising directly rather than via slow TD bootstrap across sensory latency. Workstream 0002 restructures the credit horizon: n-step returns, frame-synchronized trace updates, or decoupling the trace decay constant from `vision_stride` to bridge the 10-tick latency. Workstream 0003 diagnoses variance collapse via gradient statistics and applies shaping (normalization, TD-error scaling, trace clipping) to restore signal magnitude during foraging. Each workstream is gated independently; a single success clears the 0.70 bar.

5. **Locked constraint: homeostasis-only credit path.** All three workstreams operate within the homeostasis-only contract: no approach/avoidance shaping terms, no goal signals or shaped rewards in the TD path. Auxiliary losses (workstream 0001) are encoding-stage or self-supervised only — they never touch the TD credit path itself, preserving the constraint. Gradient shaping (workstream 0003) tunes magnitude/timing, not signal sources.

Plan 0017's simple-fix hypotheses are treated as settled-negative and are not re-litigated.

## Locked decisions

- **Homeostasis-only credit path — no shaped rewards or goal signals.** All three workstreams (0001, 0002, 0003) operate within the homeostasis-only contract inherited from plan 0012: the TD learning signal `raw_gradient = energy_delta * ENERGY_WEIGHT + integrity_delta * INTEGRITY_WEIGHT` remains the sole source of credit, with no approach/avoidance shaping terms, no goal signals, and no auxiliary shaped rewards in the TD path itself. Auxiliary losses (workstream 0001) are encoding-stage or self-supervised only — they supervise the vision representation or action encoding, not the reward signal or critic. Gradient shaping (workstream 0003) tunes magnitude/timing, not signal sources. This locks the solve to constraints: improved credit routing and signal conditioning from existing homeostatic signals, not signal design. Gate: every landed fix must pass a homeostatic-signal audit (`raw_gradient` contains only `energy_delta` and `integrity_delta` terms; auxiliary losses and gradient shaping are inspection-only, non-critical, or reversible; no new signal sources enter the TD path). Revisit: if a later experiment shows the credit bottleneck is fundamentally unfixable within homeostasis-only constraints, a new plan may propose goal-introduction with explicit discussion.

- **Steering alignment must clear 0.70 (above chance band 0.38–0.62) for a fix to be accepted.** The measurement gate for all three workstreams is the mirrored-steering probe (`learning_probe_mirrored_steering_is_chance()`, `integration.rs:2848-2929`): alignment must move from baseline 0.489 (chance band [0.38, 0.62]) above the band to ≥0.70 (ideally 0.70–0.85). Each workstream (0001 auxiliary loss, 0002 trace-horizon, 0003 gradient shaping) independently prototypes a fix, measures steering alignment, and records either ACCEPT (≥0.70) or REJECT (≤0.62) in its decision document. At least one workstream must accept for the plan to land code. If all three reject (no fix clears ≥0.70), the credit-path bottleneck is carried forward to a future plan with a new hypothesis. Gate condition: measured alignment on the mirrored-steering probe, after 120 training episodes on alternating left/right food, with movement pinned (`movement_speed=0`), must be ≥0.70. Secondary gate: steering improvement must not regress encoder separability (raycast cosine-diff ≈0.0036, Gabor ≈0.964) or food-visibility (food-consumed per episode stays >0).

- **Encoder separability and food-visibility probes must hold at baseline across all fixes.** Every fix (workstream 0001, 0002, 0003) is measured alongside two control probes to ensure no regression: (a) `encoder_food_side_separability_diagnostic()` (`integration.rs:3036-`) measures the cosine similarity between left/right encodings — the margin must stay at raycast ~0.0036 (18–24× above within-class) or Gabor ~0.964 (55× margin) unchanged, proving the encoder is not disrupted; (b) a food-visibility test (e.g. `learning_probe_baseline_turn_alignment_is_chance()` or a dedicated foraging baseline) confirms agents can still reach and eat food during training, measuring food-per-episode and asserting it stays above baseline (e.g. >100 per 120-episode training). If either probe regresses, the fix is rejected immediately. Gate: steering-improvement landing requires all three probes (steering ≥0.70, encoder separability ≈baseline, food-consumption ≈baseline) green simultaneously.

## Out of scope

- **Improving the legacy raycast encoder or raycasting throughput.** The legacy raycast path is the baseline; this plan measures fixes against it. Future optimization (ray count, culling) is out of scope; the focus is on the credit-path bottleneck, not encoder alternative paths.

- **Replicating or reverting plan 0017's false hypotheses (trace decay 0.99, urgency isolation).** These are documented as falsified in 0017's decision record. This plan attacks different mechanisms (auxiliary loss, trace-horizon, gradient variance) rather than re-testing the same hypotheses.

- **Graduated lever defaults for fitness, danger percept, or speed-cost exponent.** Flags introduced in 0009/0010/0012/0013 stay off. This plan focuses on credit unlock, not fitness-lever graduation. A follow-on plan (0021+) may revisit after this plan's verdict.

- **Multi-agent competition or environmental-pressure fitness shaping.** This is a research direction deferred to a follow-on series. Plan 0018 addresses credit unlock within homeostatic constraints, not goal-introduction via environment pressure.

- **GPU kernel restructuring or dispatch optimization.** Edits stay within existing shader functions (`coop_predict_and_act`, trace updates, weight applications). Kernel-level restructuring (0005/0006) is out of scope.

- **Intent-as-meta-score evolved-policy changes or A/B harness for encoders.** This plan does not run an encoder A/B (that was 0017 workstream 0004, superseded). The three workstreams here are credit-mechanism independent, applicable to any encoder. The intent measurement framework (0015) is consumed but not changed.

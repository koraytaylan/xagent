## Executive Summary

The method that produces "self-awareness" is a deliberately label-free, unified predictive-processing + homeostatic TD(λ) actor-critic architecture running entirely on-GPU inside a single fused dispatch. There are no hardcoded "food good / hazard bad" modules, no separate reward function, and no symbolic features. The brain sees only a flat numeric feature vector (primarily 192 RGBA + 48 depth vision pixels plus proprioception, normalized energy/integrity, deltas, and up to 4 touch contacts). All meaning — including the distinction between safe movement, food approach, and danger avoidance — must be discovered from correlations between those numbers, the agent's own exploratory motor noise, and later changes in its internal energy/integrity variables.

This is a coherent and ambitious definition of minimal autonomous intelligence. The implementation is sophisticated: a 128-dimensional encoded bottleneck, 128-pattern memory with valence-weighted recall, multi-timescale homeostasis producing an urgency-amplified scalar "reward," a learned value head with eligibility traces (TD(λ)), predictor-driven curiosity, motor fatigue, and Lamarckian inheritance of the full weight + pattern state across generations.

However, after many iterations the expected observable behaviors (consistent, cue-triggered food chasing and danger avoidance) are still weak or absent. This is **not primarily an intelligence-capacity problem**. The agents are clearly non-random and the internal machinery is adapting. The dominant issues are:

1. **Temporal desynchronization**: Sensory features (vision, touch, packed interoception) are stale by one full kernel batch (default 100 physics ticks). The policy and traces often operate on a visual snapshot that no longer matches the homeostatic outcome being credited.
2. **Death carries no learning signal**: The transition that drives energy or integrity to zero never produces a terminal TD error. Traces and episodic state are zeroed in `agent_death_respawn` (which runs before the brain pass), and the agent is immediately restored to full health at a (usually) safe location. The single strongest "danger=bad" event teaches nothing.
3. **Asymmetric sensory grounding**: Food has multiple timely paths (lime visual hit, direct `TOUCH_FOOD` contact, immediate +energy on consumption). Danger has only the reddish terrain color in vision plus a slow per-tick integrity drain while inside a `BIOME_DANGER` cell. Critically, the GPU `phase_vision_senses` never emits `TOUCH_HAZARD` or `TOUCH_TERRAIN_EDGE` even though the constants and the CPU reference path define them.
4. **Homeostatic deltas are the sole teacher, and they are mostly small/sparse**: Food is a +20 energy burst (≈ +0.2 normalized). Metabolic drain and hazard damage are continuous and small. The critic must bootstrap across the gap using traces whose effective per-dimension step is tiny (`CRITIC_LEARNING_RATE * TD_VECTOR_SCALE ≈ 8e-5`).
5. **Evolution can succeed without selecting for within-lifetime learning**: Composite fitness (`survival*0.4 + foraging*0.3 + exploration*0.3`) plus persistence of `food_count`/`ticks_alive` across free respawns can be satisfied by competent random walking + klinotaxis + initial forward bias. The outer loop therefore does not strongly pressure the system to evolve fast cue-to-action binding.

The survival-pressure mechanism exists (homeo gradient → urgency → reward + exploration suppression). What is missing for rapid emergence is sufficiently tight, low-latency, causally reliable coupling between the sensory cues the agent actually sees at decision time and the homeostatic consequences of the actions it takes.

## The Learning Method in Detail

All persistent adaptation lives in two GPU buffers per agent:
- `brain_state_buf` (`BrainLayout::brain_stride`, vision-dependent; 51 898 f32 at default 8×6) — encoder weights, predictor, habituation EMAs/attenuation, homeostasis state, forward/turn policy weights + biases, value head weights + bias, eligibility traces (episodic), exploration/fatigue state, heritable config scalars.
- `pattern_buf` (`PATTERN_STRIDE = 17 539 f32`, fixed) — 128 patterns, each storing encoded state, L2 norm, reinforcement, stored motor + outcome valence, metadata, active flag.

The seven cooperative stages (inlined into the per-agent workgroup in `kernel_tick.wgsl` and `brain_tick.wgsl`) execute every brain tick:

1. `coop_feature_extract` — copies vision RGBA + depth from the lagged `sensory_buffer` plus 25 non-visual scalars (speed, facing, angular vel, normalized energy/integrity, deltas, 4× touch contacts). Produces the 265-dim `s_features` (default 8×6).
2. `coop_encode` — linear projection + tanh into 128-dim `s_encoded` (Xavier-initialized encoder).
3. `coop_habituate_homeo` — per-dimension EMA of change for habituation/attenuation/curiosity; three-timescale EMAs of raw homeo gradient + urgency from current normalized energy/integrity read directly from `physics_state` (same-cycle). Writes `s_homeo[6]` and updates brain state. This is the only place "good/bad" is computed.
4. `coop_recall_score` + `coop_recall_topk` — cosine similarity (on pre-habituation encoded state) against up to 128 patterns; subgroup or bitonic top-16 selection. Updates pattern meta (last_accessed, activation_count).
5. `coop_predict_and_act` (the heart of credit assignment):
   - Predictor matmul on habituated state (+ recalled-context blend) → `s_prediction`.
   - RMSE prediction error vs. previous prediction (drives novelty exploration and is stored in ring).
   - Value head reduction over `s_encoded` → `v`.
   - `reward = s_homeo[1u]` (raw_gradient_amplified by urgency).
   - `δ = clamp(reward + TD_DISCOUNT * v - prev_value, -MAX_TD_ERROR, MAX_TD_ERROR)`.
   - Immediate bias updates and per-dimension weight updates through traces for critic, forward policy, and turn policy.
   - `s_credit[d] = td_error * (forward_trace[d] + turn_trace[d])` for later encoder credit.
   - Linear policy: `fwd/turn = bias + dot(weights, s_encoded)`.
   - Memory motor blend using stored valence (positive → reproduce, negative → invert; up to 40% contribution).
   - Dynamic exploration rate (novelty + curiosity from mean attenuation - urgency penalty).
   - Motor fatigue from position-ring staleness + motor variance.
   - **Klinotaxis** (hardcoded prior): `turn *= clamp(1.0 - (grad_fast - grad_med) * KLINOTAXIS_SENSITIVITY, 0.3, 3.0)`.
   - Publish pre-fatigue/pre-klinotaxis exploration noise for trace update; write decision buffer and telemetry.
6. Eligibility trace update (parallel): `z ← TD_DISCOUNT*TD_LAMBDA*z + feature_term` (encoded for critic, noise*encoded for actors).
7. `coop_learn_and_store`:
   - Predictor gradient descent (tanh derivative, clipped).
   - Context-weight adaptation.
   - Encoder Hebbian credit: features that co-occurred with strong `action_credit` get their weights nudged (scaled by `ENCODER_CREDIT_SCALE = 0.1`).
   - Memory reinforcement for active patterns with sim > 0.3: `reinf += sim * lr * (1 - pred_error)`; retroactive valence EMA toward current raw_gradient.
   - Unconditional store of current pre-habituation encoded state + current motor + current raw_gradient into the weakest (min-reinf) slot.
   - Decay (frequency- and recency-modulated); deactivate when reinf ≤ 0; update min-reinf index.

Constants that matter for credit speed and horizon (all in `common.wgsl`):
- `TD_DISCOUNT = 0.97`, `TD_LAMBDA = 0.9`
- `CRITIC_LEARNING_RATE = 0.01`, `ACTION_WEIGHT_LEARNING_RATE = 0.10`
- `TD_VECTOR_SCALE = 1.0 / 128`
- `KLINOTAXIS_SENSITIVITY = 500.0`
- `MAX_HOMEOSTATIC_DELTA = 0.3`, energy weight 0.6 / integrity 0.4
- Homeo blends: fast 0.6, med 0.04, slow 0.004 (effective windows ~5/50/500 brain ticks)

Traces and `prev_value` are zeroed on death (episodic). Value weights, policy weights, encoder, predictor, and pattern valence survive and are the heritable "knowledge."

## How Food and Danger Actually Reach the Learner

**Food path (relatively strong):**
- Raycast can hit food → bright lime `(0.7, 0.95, 0.2)` pixel in vision (food-rich terrain is darker green `(0.15, 0.5, 0.1)`).
- 3×3 cell touch scan can produce `TOUCH_FOOD` (tag=1) contacts with direction/proximity.
- Consumption (atomic claim in food-detect inside the stride physics loop) adds `WC_FOOD_ENERGY` (default 20) directly to `P_ENERGY`. This appears as a positive energy_delta the next time `coop_habituate_homeo` runs.
- The positive raw_gradient becomes the TD reward and is also stored as valence on the pattern that happened to be written that tick.

**Danger path (noticeably weaker):**
- Raycast on DANGER biome terrain → reddish `(0.6, 0.2, 0.1)`.
- While inside a `BIOME_DANGER` cell, `phase_physics` (or `agent_physics`) subtracts `WC_HAZARD_DAMAGE * integrity_scale` from integrity every physics sub-tick.
- No `TOUCH_HAZARD` is ever written by the live GPU `phase_vision_senses` (only FOOD and AGENT; see lines 259-267 and 299-307 in phase_vision.wgsl). The constants `TOUCH_HAZARD=3` and `TOUCH_TERRAIN_EDGE=2` exist in common.wgsl:213-214 and the CPU reference path emits them, but the brain never receives the tag on the critical path.
- The negative integrity delta produces negative raw_gradient → negative TD reward, but only after the agent has already been inside the zone for some time, and only via the (lagged) interoceptive channel.

**Interoception timing split (intentional but consequential):**
- `coop_habituate_homeo` (the reward source) reads current normalized energy/integrity + previous values *directly from `physics_state`* in the same cycle (brain_passes.wgsl:149-157).
- The 25 non-visual feature slots that go into `s_features` (and therefore the encoder, policy, value head, traces, and recall) come from the *previous* vision batch via `sensory_buffer` (phase_vision_senses packs them at 206-222).
- Result: the "what state am I in" vector the policy sees can be up to 100 ticks old, while the scalar reward/urgency uses fresher homeo numbers. The kernel comments in kernel_tick.wgsl:515-523 explicitly flag this as the sensory-lag risk for credit assignment.

## Death and the Missing Terminal Lesson

In the per-cycle loop (kernel_tick.wgsl:474-526):
- stride physics ticks (including food consumption and biome damage)
- `agent_food_detect`
- `agent_death_respawn` (if `P_DIED_FLAG`)
- `brain_tick_inner` (the 7 passes)

`agent_death_respawn` (extracted from phase_death.wgsl) does:
- Safe (or fallback danger) spawn search
- Preserve fitness counters, reset physics to full energy/integrity
- Halve all pattern `O_PAT_REINF`
- Zero homeo EMAs, habituation, exploration rate, pos ring, fatigue, **all three eligibility trace vectors + trace biases + prev_value**

Because this runs *before* `brain_tick_inner`, and because the brain pass only runs if the (now-respawned) agent is alive, the transition that actually drove energy/integrity ≤ 0 never participates in a TD update. The eligibility traces that should have carried blame for the fatal actions are zeroed without ever seeing the terminal negative δ. The agent wakes up "feeling great" (full resources) at a new location.

This is the single largest missing survival signal for "danger=bad."

## Evolution Does Not Strongly Select for the Desired Learning

From governor.rs:471-479 (absolute scoring, no normalization):
```rust
let survival = 1.0 / (1.0 + r.death_count as f32 * 0.5);
let foraging = (r.food_consumed as f32 / food_target).min(1.0);
let exploration = (r.cells_explored as f32 / total_grid_cells).min(1.0);
r.composite_fitness = survival * 0.4 + foraging * 0.3 + exploration * 0.3;
```

`food_consumed` and `ticks_alive` are preserved through respawn (kernel_tick and phase_death). The initial forward bias (`buffers.rs:34`, `INITIAL_FORWARD_BIAS = 0.3`), exploration noise, fatigue, and klinotaxis already generate non-zero movement and occasional accidental eating. A population can therefore achieve respectable composite scores (and be selected as parents) while the within-lifetime policy remains close to random with respect to specific visual or touch cues. The inherited brain state includes whatever weak associations were learned, but the selection pressure does not explicitly reward *improvement rate* inside a single life.

## Direct Answers to the Embedded Questions

**Is it maybe an intelligence problem?**

No. The architecture implements a non-trivial, capacity-constrained, grounded learner: predictive compression into 128 dims, finite episodic memory with reinforcement and valence, TD(λ) bootstrapping + traces that can in principle bridge multi-step delays, curiosity from habituation, urgency-modulated exploration, and cross-generation inheritance of the learned weights themselves. Agents demonstrably adapt (prediction error drops, policies become less noisy, memories are reinforced or decay). The capacity is not the binding constraint for the behaviors the user expects.

**Are we failing to define intelligence properly?**

The definition articulated in the xagent-brain README ("Prediction error drives everything" + homeostatic gradient as the universal evaluative signal, no hand-crafted goals, emergence under hard constraints on memory/attention/representation) is internally consistent and philosophically clean. It correctly rejects the usual shortcuts. What has been missing until the 2026-06-10 baseline work is an *operational* measurement of that definition — direct probes of whether a visual feature has become causally linked to a homeostatic outcome and whether the policy now acts on that link (e.g., turn alignment to visible food, hazard exit latency, within-generation improvement in foraging rate). Aggregate population fitness is a useful evolutionary filter but a blunt instrument for diagnosing lifetime learning speed.

**Is it maybe about the survival instinct what they are lacking?**

They possess a survival *pressure* system. Low or falling energy/integrity produces negative raw_gradient, raises urgency, increases the magnitude of the TD reward (in the painful direction), suppresses exploration, and (via klinotaxis) biases turns toward improving homeo gradient. This is the functional equivalent of an instinctual "this internal state is bad, do something." 

What they lack for fast, reliable expression of that pressure is:
- Timely, unambiguous sensory evidence of the *current* dangerous or rewarding situation (lagged features + absent hazard touch tags).
- Credit for the terminal consequence (death).
- Possibly additional low-level reactive scaffolding if the goal is observable behavior in dozens-to-hundreds of ticks rather than evolutionary timescales.

The design philosophy ("they must figure it out on their own") is respected, but the data the agent experiences does not yet make the figure-it-out problem easy enough for the credit-assignment machinery to solve at a human-visible rate.

## Additional Mechanical Notes Worth Tracking

- Predictor training (pass 7a) regresses the current prediction against the current habituated state, while the scalar prediction error used for exploration and memory reinforcement compares the *previous* prediction against the current state. The training target is not exactly the quantity being measured.
- The TD_DISCOUNT comment claims a ~33-brain-tick horizon matches "travel time from the edge of vision range"; at default stride=10 the actual horizon is much shorter relative to travel time.
- Exploration noise is published *before* fatigue and klinotaxis scaling, so the traces credit a larger action than the one the physics step actually executed.
- Every brain tick unconditionally stores a pattern (valence ≈ metabolic drain most of the time). Memory pressure and decay must do a lot of the filtering.
- Depth channels are written to sensory_buffer but dropped from the feature vector (feature_count = color + depth + 25, but the 25 non-visual slot already accounts for the non-visual tail; depth pixels themselves are not turned into derived features).

## Relationship to Existing Specs, Plans, and Reviews

This analysis is consistent with the four 2026-06-12 reviews and directly supports the concrete grounding fixes enumerated in the 2026-06-12 "Survival Signal Grounding" plan (terminal TD update on death before trace zeroing, emission of `TOUCH_HAZARD`/`TOUCH_TERRAIN_EDGE` from the live GPU vision path, same-cycle interoception into `s_features` for the encoder/policy/value head, hazard-specific probe + quarter-split within-lifetime metrics, observability of the non-visual sensory tail, etc.). The 2026-06-10 learning-baseline spec already provides the measurement discipline (deterministic probes, before/after numbers, merge gates) that should be extended for danger avoidance and learning-rate-within-life.

---

## Bottom Line

The agents *are* self-aware in the narrow but important sense that their value estimates, policy weights, encoder, predictor, and valence-tagged memories are continuously shaped by their own history of prediction errors and homeostatic outcomes, and that knowledge is preserved and transmitted. The reason we do not yet see consistent, cue-conditioned danger avoidance and food pursuit is that the causal chain from "I see this color pattern / feel this touch" through action to "my energy/integrity changed because of it" is too long, too lagged, too asymmetrically grounded, and (for death) completely severed for the existing TD(λ) + trace machinery.

This is a grounding, timing, and measurement problem that the current sophisticated learner is in principle capable of solving once the world it experiences supplies clearer, more timely correlations. The active survival-signal-grounding work is aimed exactly at supplying those correlations while preserving the "no labels, discover it yourself" philosophy. Once those changes land behind the existing probe gates, we should be able to measure whether the expected behaviors appear at useful rates.

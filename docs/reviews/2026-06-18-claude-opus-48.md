## Executive summary

Plan 0009's **mechanical implementation is solid**. The four layers are wired
into both the fused (`kernel_tick.wgsl`) and split (`phase_physics.wgsl` /
`phase_death.wgsl`) execution paths with genuinely strong byte-identical-at-defaults
discipline, an exhaustive Rust↔WGSL constant-parity harness for the new physics
slots, and a full-physics-slice fused/split equality test that is actually
exercised by a danger-rich seed-42 world. The shipping **default build is a
verified no-op** in output: drag falls back to the exact old expression at
`k=1.0`, `effort_rebased_fitness=false` reproduces the old time-denominated
scores, and `danger_percept_enabled=false` leaves the feature count and encoded
brain state unchanged. The plan correctly holds the default-flip GATED rather
than flipping on questionable validation numbers — the right call.

The work is **not ready to graduate the flags**, for two distinct reasons that
the three prior reports did not connect:

1. **The effort-rebased calibration is latently miscalibrated at production
   scale (the keystone finding, HIGH).** `FORAGING_ENERGY_TARGET=0.5` and
   `EXPLORATION_DISTANCE_BUDGET=16.0` were picked from *synthetic per-life*
   profiles, but the real `energy_spent` and `distance_traveled` are
   **cumulative-per-generation GPU accumulators preserved across respawn**, so
   over a ~1M-tick generation they are 100–1000× larger than the synthetic
   numbers. Both effort axes therefore collapse toward a near-constant ~0.02 for
   the entire population, defeating Layer C's whole purpose ("skill outscores raw
   speed"). The validation doc's own **84% ON mean-fitness collapse (0.0779 →
   0.0122)** is direct evidence. This is dormant behind `effort_rebased_fitness=false`,
   so it ships no harm today — but it is exactly the regression that would land
   the moment someone flips the flag on the strength of the current calibration.

2. **The decision machinery overclaims its evidence (MEDIUM cluster).** The
   speed-decoupling gate marks **PASS while its own generated doc says the
   primary metric rose the wrong way** (correlation 0.2238 → 0.2569, +14.8%); the
   A/B is uncontrolled (unseeded per-arm RNG in three places); two of the three
   gate conjuncts are uninformative (one tautologically true and *excluded* from
   the pass condition, one saturated by the 1M-tick budget); and the entire
   statistical core + gate has **zero unit tests**.

**Grade: mechanically strong, ship-ready at defaults (no-op verified); multiple
blockers before graduating any flag.** No blocking issue exists in the shipped
default path. The blockers are all on the path to flipping `effort_rebased_fitness`
or `danger_percept_enabled` on. **Do not flip defaults until the calibration is
re-derived from real recorded telemetry (or made scale-invariant) and the
gate/harness are corrected.**

## Findings

Severity: **HIGH** (1) · **MEDIUM** (7) · **LOW** (10) · **NIT** (6). Each marks
its relationship to the prior 2026-06-18 reports as **NEW** / **AGREE** /
**EXTEND** / **CONTRADICT**.

### HIGH

#### H1. Effort-rebased foraging axis collapses to ~0 at production scale
**`governor.rs` `composite_fitness()` (≈129–141; `FORAGING_ENERGY_TARGET=0.5` ≈52); accumulation `kernel_tick.wgsl:189` / `phase_physics.wgsl:143`; respawn-preserve `phase_death.wgsl:86`. — NEW (root cause behind a finding Gemini & Grok only saw as a weak test).**

The foraging axis is `min((food_consumed / energy_spent) / FORAGING_ENERGY_TARGET, 1.0)`.
`energy_spent` is a GPU accumulator summing depletion + movement drain **every
tick and preserved across respawn** (`phase_death.wgsl:86`), so over a ~1M-tick
generation it reaches O(15,000–65,000); depletion alone is
`energy_depletion_rate(0.03) × metabolic_rate(0.5) = 0.015/tick × ~1,000,000 ≈
15,000`, and the validation doc's own mean `ticks_alive ≈ 999,132` corroborates
the magnitude. `food_consumed` is a `+1.0`-per-item count in the low hundreds.
So real `food/energy ≈ few-hundred / ~15,000 ≈ 0.003–0.02`, giving foraging ≈
0.02 for essentially the whole population. The calibration
(`0009-FITNESS-CALIBRATION.md`) assumed `food/energy = 180/180 = 1.0`, a ratio
production never produces. The weight-0.85 dominant axis is therefore pinned near
zero; the validation doc's 84% ON mean-fitness collapse is the smoking gun.

The axis keeps weak monotonicity (a more-efficient forager still scores
*marginally* higher), so this is heavy compression rather than a literal hard
zero — and it is dormant behind `effort_rebased_fitness=false`. But it defeats
the headline objective at production tick budgets.

**Recommendation:** Do **not** flip the effort flag with the current constant.
Either re-derive `FORAGING_ENERGY_TARGET` from real recorded telemetry (the
spec's generation-recording replay that was skipped — see M1), or make the axis
scale-invariant by normalizing `energy_spent` by `ticks_alive`. Add a
production-scale assertion that a competent forager reaches foraging ≈ 1.0 on
real telemetry, and treat the 84% ON-fitness collapse as a **failing** signal,
not a passing one.

### MEDIUM

#### M1. Calibration constants chosen from synthetic profiles, not the spec-mandated recorded-generation replay
**`0009-FITNESS-CALIBRATION.md` (Deviation §143–165); synthetic profiles `governor.rs:2359–2384`; spec step `TASKS.md:255–256`. — EXTEND of Gemini & Grok (both flagged the test as print-only); NEW in connecting the deviation to the production scale mismatch.**

`TASKS.md` step 1 explicitly requires replaying a recorded generation's telemetry
through old-vs-new `composite_fitness`. The landed calibration instead uses three
synthetic profiles whose energy (180, 800, 60) and distance (600, 4000, 1) are
invented per-life numbers, and the doc declares them "the source of truth"
(line 165). The synthetic energy is even self-inconsistent: its own
`ticks=100,000` at `0.015/tick` already implies ~1,500 energy, not 180. The
skipped recorded-generation replay is exactly the check that would have surfaced
the scale mismatch in H1/M2. There is a backstop — a later task recomputes
`food_per_energy` from real cumulative telemetry before any flip — so impact is
dormant.

**Recommendation:** Run the spec's `generation_recording` replay (the
`store_recording`/`load_recording` machinery already exists) and re-pick both
constants from real distributions, or make the formulas per-tick-rate
scale-invariant. Block the default-flip until calibration is validated against
real telemetry.

#### M2. Effort-rebased exploration axis also collapses; the "non-binding" distance budget is actually the binding constraint
**`governor.rs` `composite_fitness()` (≈135–139; `EXPLORATION_DISTANCE_BUDGET=16.0` ≈62); accumulation `kernel_tick.wgsl:162` / `phase_physics.wgsl:118`; respawn-preserve `phase_death.wgsl:85`. — NEW; directly contradicts the calibration doc's "non-binding guard" claim.**

`cells_per_dist = min(cells_explored / (distance_traveled / 16.0), 1.0)`;
`exploration = min(coverage, cells_per_dist)`. `distance_traveled` accumulates
`step_len` every tick and is preserved across respawn, reaching O(hundreds of
thousands) over a generation, while `cells_explored` is capped at the reachable
grid (~1024). At baseline 20 units/s, dt=1/30 → ~0.667 units/tick × 1M ≈ 667,000
units, so `cells_per_dist ≈ 1024/(667000/16) ≈ 0.025` and exploration ≈ 0.025 for
every agent. The calibration doc (lines 42–46) calls the budget a "non-binding
guard" — true only for the synthetic single-life distances 600/4000/1. In
production it is the **dominant** binding constraint and removes the axis's
discriminating power (weight 0.15).

**Recommendation:** Same fix family as H1 — normalize distance by `ticks_alive`
or life count, or set the budget against real recorded distance; verify on real
telemetry that exploration does not pin to ~0 across the population.

#### M3. `P_ENERGY_SPENT` under-counts true energy: per-tick brain metabolic drain is subtracted from energy but never accumulated into the telemetry slot
**`kernel_tick.wgsl` agent_physics (≈184–189 + 228) and `phase_physics.wgsl` (≈138–143 + 178). — NEW.**

The accumulator that feeds the foraging axis records only baseline depletion +
movement drain. The per-tick brain metabolic drain
`(METABOLIC_BASE_COST + mem_cap·METABOLIC_MEMORY_COST + proc_slots·METABOLIC_PROCESSING_COST)·metabolic_rate`
is subtracted from real energy (line 228) **without** being added to
`P_ENERGY_SPENT`. The omission is identical in both paths (no parity drift), and
`metabolic_rate` cancels in the food/energy ratio. At `BrainConfig::default()`
(memory 128, processing 16, depletion 0.03) the omitted term is ≈18% of
depletion drain; at `BrainConfig::large()` (memory 512, processing 32) it is
≈62%. So the readback systematically understates burned energy, and the
under-count **scales with brain size** — two agents with identical food and
movement but different brains get different (mis-ranked) foraging scores, a
brain-size confound that undercuts the "effort-rebased = brain-agnostic" intent.
(Bounded by the `.min(1.0)` cap, so it only bites uncapped agents.)

**Recommendation:** Add the brain metabolic drain to `P_ENERGY_SPENT` in **both**
shaders (accumulate the full per-tick energy delta), keeping the paths
byte-identical, then re-run calibration — or explicitly document that
`energy_spent` excludes fixed brain overhead and adjust the calibration profiles.

#### M4. Avoidance-intent metric has an inverted sign convention: it counts turning TOWARD danger as avoidance
**`kernel_tick.wgsl` agent_physics (≈254–261) + agent_danger_detect bearing (≈316–321); `phase_physics.wgsl` (≈257–264 / 230–235). — NEW (Gemini & GPT-5 flagged a *temporal staleness* in these counters; none caught the sign inversion).**

The "turn opposes bearing" test treats opposite signs of `motor_turn` and
`danger_bearing` as a deliberate turn-away, but the two quantities use **opposite
sign conventions**. `bearing = atan2(facing_x·to_danger.z − facing_z·to_danger.x,
dot)`. With yaw=0 (facing=+z) and danger on the agent's right (+x),
`to_danger=(1,0,0)` gives cross_y = `0·0 − 1·1 = −1`, dot=0, so bearing =
`atan2(−1,0) = −π/2` (**negative** for right-side danger). Positive `motor_turn`
rotates toward +x (right). So a *correct* avoidance of right-side danger is a left
turn (`motor_turn<0`): `motor_turn·bearing = (−)(−) = +`, which is **not**
counted; turning right *into* the danger gives `(+)(−) = −`, which **is** counted
as "opposing." The in-source comments ("positive = danger to the right") are also
backwards. Duplicated verbatim in both paths.

These counters flow only into `avoidance_intent_fraction` (observability
telemetry) — they are **not** passed to `composite_fitness` (`governor.rs:659–668`),
so selection/reward are uncorrupted. But the metric reports the **opposite of the
truth**: good avoiders score ~0, agents that steer into hazards score high — and
`avoidance_intent_fraction` is a named criterion the plan's gate is meant to
preserve. The existing unit test sets the counters directly on the CPU `Agent`
struct, so it cannot catch the kernel inversion; `nearest_danger_bearing_points_at_danger`
only asserts the bearing is non-zero (see M7).

**Recommendation:** Fix the sign so a genuine turn-away increments the counter
(negate the bearing convention, or flip the test to `> 0.0` against the corrected
convention), identically in both shaders. Add a GPU-level test that drives a known
`motor_turn` against a known left/right danger placement and asserts the counter
increments only for the away-turn.

#### M5. Speed-decoupling gate marks PASS when the primary metric moved the wrong way; the generated doc literally self-contradicts
**`headless.rs` `format_validation_markdown` gate (≈976–980); criterion text vs `corr_direction` (≈627, 672–673, 998–999); doc `0009-SPEED-DECOUPLING.md:16,37,67`. — EXTEND of GPT-5 Codex P1 and Gemini Medium (proves the doc self-contradicts).**

The gate is `speed_decoupled = on_stats.speed_fitness_correlation.abs() < 0.3`,
with no requirement that the baseline be strongly positive and no requirement that
ON < baseline; `gate_passed = speed_decoupled && ticks_alive_ok && danger_retained`
(line 980). `corr_delta`/`corr_direction` feed only prose, never the gate. The
committed decision doc prints criterion 1 as "Speed-fitness correlation falls from
strongly-positive to approx 0 (|r|<0.3): PASS" while the same doc's Analysis says
"the speed-fitness correlation **rose** from 0.2238 (baseline) to 0.2569 (ON), a
14.8% change." The metric the plan exists to reduce **increased**, the baseline
was already below 0.3, yet the doc declares decoupling and GATE PASSED. This is
offline advisory tooling (no automatic runtime flip), so harm requires a human
acting on a misleading report — but the report is internally inconsistent on its
face.

**Recommendation:** Make the gate require (a) a strongly-positive baseline
(`baseline_corr ≥ 0.3`) and (b) strict improvement (`on_corr.abs() < baseline_corr.abs()`
by a margin). Until then, criterion-1 text must not print PASS when
`corr_direction == "rose"`.

#### M6. Speed-decoupling A/B is uncontrolled: baseline and ON arms draw independent unseeded RNG in three places
**`headless.rs` `run_headless_with_flags` (mutate_config / mutate_brain_state / reset_agents); `agent/mod.rs:378,500`; `gpu_kernel.rs:523`. — EXTEND of GPT-5 Codex P1 and Gemini Medium (adds `reset_agents` as a third source).**

Both arms come from separate `run_headless_with_flags` calls, each seeding the
population via `mutate_config` (`rand::rng()`, `agent/mod.rs:378`), mutating
inherited brain state via `mutate_brain_state` (`rand::rng()`, `agent/mod.rs:500`),
and reinitializing brain weights every generation via `kernel.reset_agents`
(`rand::rng()`, `gpu_kernel.rs:523`). None are seeded per-arm; a deterministic
`reset_agents_seeded` exists but the harness deliberately does not use it. The
world (terrain/food/spawn) **is** seeded and identical across arms, so the
confound is scoped to genome perturbations and brain weights — but it means
cross-arm deltas (mean fitness 0.0779 → 0.0122; `ticks_alive_ok = on > baseline·0.8`)
cannot be attributed to the flags, even though the harness markets itself as "the
canonical baseline for all future Plan 0009 A/B tests" (`headless.rs:1095`).
Mitigation: the keystone within-arm decoupling gate (correlation over the ON arm's
own pooled agents) is largely immune to cross-arm RNG, so the *primary* gate is
more robust than the cross-arm deltas it also reports.

**Recommendation:** Thread a fixed seed (e.g. derived from `config.world.seed`)
through all three sites so the arms share identical random draws and the only
difference is the flags — making it a paired comparison. The seeded reset entry
point already exists.

#### M7. `nearest_danger_bearing_points_at_danger` never asserts the bearing points at the danger for the in-range agent — it only prints it
**`tests/integration.rs` `nearest_danger_bearing_points_at_danger` (read ≈6464–6501). — NEW.**

The test's titular property is never asserted for the in-range agent.
`agent0_bearing` is read and only `eprintln!`'d; the only assertions on agent0 are
on **distance** (`< SENTINEL && > 0.0`). Bearing is asserted only for agent1, the
**far** agent, where it must equal the sentinel `0.0`. A regression producing a
finite danger distance but a wrong/garbage/NaN bearing — exactly the failure this
plan-named acceptance criterion (`TASKS.md:285–286`, `ARCHITECTURE.md:435–436`) is
meant to guard, and which Layer D's avoidance shaping consumes — would pass. The
setup is deterministic (seed-42 facing, agent at (0,y,2.0), danger at origin), so
an assertion was feasible; note the shader computes a *facing-relative signed*
bearing, not world −Z, so the expected value must be computed accordingly. (This
is the test-coverage twin of M4 — together they explain why the sign inversion
shipped undetected.)

**Recommendation:** Assert the near agent's bearing falls in the expected angular
window pointing from the agent toward the danger cell (compute the analytic
facing-relative bearing, assert within a band); at minimum assert
finite/non-sentinel.

### LOW

#### L1. Calibration test asserts ordering only, not the documented deltas/magnitudes
**`governor.rs` `fitness_calibration_replay_profiles` asserts (≈2503–2516). — CONTRADICT-then-EXTEND of Gemini & Grok ("print-only, no asserts").**

A follow-up commit (`916a25ad`) added two asserts — `effort_competent > effort_aimless`
and `effort_camper < 1.0` — so the prior "no asserts" claim is now **stale**. The
residual: only ordering and one one-sided bound are pinned. The doc's headline
numbers (competent 0.7050, fast-aimless 0.3656, delta −0.3506, gap 0.3394, camper
0.8650) appear nowhere in the source, so a regression that halved the fast-aimless
penalty or shifted anchors while preserving `competent>aimless` and `camper<1.0`
would still pass. The behaviorally load-bearing invariants are locked; the
documented magnitudes are not.

**Recommendation:** Pin the documented composites/deltas to a tolerance (e.g.
`(effort_aimless − 0.3656).abs() < 1e-3`, `(gap − 0.3394).abs() < 1e-3`) so a math
regression that preserves ordering still fails.

#### L2. Danger/avoidance fraction reductions differ between production persistence (sum/sum) and the validation harness (mean-of-clamped-ratios)
**`governor.rs` `persist_behavior_metrics` (≈829–861) vs `headless.rs` (≈790–822). — AGREE with Gemini (Low) and GPT-5 Codex (P2).**

The governor persists `danger_dwell_fraction = total_danger_path / max(total_distance, EPS)`
and `avoidance_intent_fraction = total_turns_opposing / max(total_sense_range_ticks, EPS)`
— a population sum/sum with no per-agent clamp. The harness instead averages
per-agent ratios with `.min(1.0)` on dwell and a `.max(1.0)` sense-ticks floor.
These are different statistics and diverge under skewed per-agent distributions;
the headless comment "mirroring governor.rs:843–844" is inaccurate. Observability
only — the gate uses bounds, not equality, so divergence cannot flip it.

**Recommendation:** Pick one reduction (sum/sum is the more defensible population
statistic) and share it between the two files, as `quarter_food_rates` is already
shared.

#### L3. The avoidance-intent gate criterion is trivially true AND excluded from `gate_passed`
**`headless.rs` gate (≈979–980). — EXTEND of L2; NEW sub-finding.**

`avoidance_retained = on_stats.mean_avoidance_intent_fraction >= 0.0` is always
true (the code comment says so: "non-negative by construction") **and** is not in
`gate_passed` (line 980 uses only `speed_decoupled && ticks_alive_ok &&
danger_retained`). The named "danger-decision-retention" criterion the layer
exists to preserve neither uses a correct (non-inverted — see M4) metric nor
constrains the flip; it only affects a cosmetic PASS string.

**Recommendation:** After fixing the M4 sign inversion, replace the trivially-true
check with a meaningful threshold (avoidance above a floor AND above chance) and
actually include it in `gate_passed`.

#### L4. Population-viability gate (`ticks_alive ≥ 80% of baseline`) is uninformative: both arms saturate the 1M-tick budget
**`headless.rs` `ticks_alive_ok` (≈977); `tick_budget` default `config.rs:429`. — EXTEND (plan open-risks note saturation in prose; no peer report flagged the gate component itself).**

Recorded means are 998,817 (baseline) and 999,132 (ON), both within 0.1% of
`tick_budget=1,000,000`. Because death-and-respawn happen in the same tick and
`P_TICKS_ALIVE` is preserved across respawn, total `ticks_alive` is structurally
pinned to ~`(tick_budget − total_deaths)` in every arm. So one of three
GATE-PASSED conjuncts carries no signal at this budget — at best a
catastrophic-collapse floor, not a viability sensitivity test. "GATE PASSED"
effectively rests on only the correlation and danger-retention conjuncts.

**Recommendation:** Measure viability with an uncapped metric (death_count per
generation, or mean longest-life), or run with `tick_budget` far below the typical
survival horizon so the 80% floor can bind.

#### L5. The entire speed-decoupling decision machinery has zero unit tests
**`headless.rs` `compute_correlation` (≈841), `compute_regression` (≈871), `format_validation_markdown`/gate (≈975–980); only caller is CLI-gated. — NEW.**

These functions are referenced only from `validate_speed_decoupling`, invoked only
behind `--validate-speed-decoupling`. Two greps show no `#[test]`/`#[cfg(test)]`
referencing them, `ValidationStats`, or the gate variables. The component that
gates a default-flip is itself unverified: a regression in the correlation math
(denominator guard, numerator accumulation) or the boolean conjunction would not
be caught by `cargo test`. CLI-only advisory tooling, off the production path — so
a defect misleads a person during a deliberate run, it does not silently corrupt
shipped behavior.

**Recommendation:** Add GPU-free unit tests: (1) `compute_correlation` returns ±1
on perfectly (anti)correlated vectors and 0 on constant x; (2) the gate returns
NOT-PASSED when `on_corr > baseline_corr`, when ticks collapse, and when danger
goes to zero.

#### L6. Danger biome ring-scan runs unconditionally even with `danger_percept_enabled` off — default build is byte-identical in OUTPUT but not in COMPUTE
**`kernel_tick.wgsl` agent_danger_detect (≈269–334, called unconditionally at ≈803–805); `phase_physics.wgsl` (≈197–265). — EXTEND of Grok ("unmeasured perf interaction").**

`agent_danger_detect` performs an `O((ceil(DANGER_SENSE_RADIUS/cell)+1)^2)`
biome-grid scan plus `atan2` every cycle, and increments the avoidance counters,
**regardless of `danger_percept_enabled`** — the flag only gates feature packing
and the reward term, not the scan (`kernel_tick.wgsl:803` has no flag guard). At
default `world_size=200` the biome cell is ~0.781 units, so `max_cell_delta =
ceil(30/0.781)+1 = 40` and the loop is `(2·40+1)² = 6561` iterations of storage
fetch + arithmetic, serial on thread 0 — larger than prior estimates. Correctness
and determinism are unaffected; the default-off build just pays a permanent
per-tick cost the "byte-identical no-op" framing can obscure. `ARCHITECTURE.md`
scopes byte-identicality to encoded state and names the scan as a tracked
throughput risk, but STATUS Verification is Pending and no measured delta is
recorded — relevant given the `2026-06-15-brain-pass-latency-ceiling.md` finding
that the fused brain/kernel pass is already the throughput ceiling at small N.

**Recommendation:** Gate the entire scan behind `WC_DANGER_PERCEPT_ENABLED` so the
default build is a true no-op, OR record the measured throughput delta against the
0005/0006 baseline and state explicitly that the default build is byte-identical
in OUTPUT but not COMPUTE.

#### L7. Calibration doc/test use grid=1000 but production `evaluate()` uses `total_grid_cells=1024`
**doc `0009-FITNESS-CALIBRATION.md:81` ("600/1000"); test `governor.rs:2352` (`grid=1000.0`) vs production `governor.rs:656`. — NEW.**

`evaluate()` computes `total_grid_cells = HEATMAP_RES²/4 = 64²/4 = 1024`, but the
calibration doc and `fitness_calibration_replay_profiles` use 1000.0. The
competent-forager coverage is `600/1024 = 0.586` in production, not 0.60, shifting
the documented composite ~0.0016. Since `cells_per_dist` saturates to 1.0 for
these profiles, `exploration = coverage`, so the mismatched denominator directly
drives the printed value. Sub-0.002 composite impact, no production effect.

**Recommendation:** Use the real production denominator (1024) in the test and
doc, or document the 1000 rounding explicitly.

#### L8. `recorded_telemetry_persists_in_agent_fitness` tests its own in-test copy loop, never `AgentFitness` or any persistence path
**`tests/integration.rs` (read ≈6371–6389). — NEW (distinct from Grok's DB-audit note).**

Despite its name, the test never constructs/reads an `AgentFitness`, never calls
`governor.evaluate`, and never touches the DB. It copies GPU buffer slots into
agent-struct fields inside the test body
(`agents[i].distance_traveled = state[base + P_DISTANCE_TRAVELED]`) and then
asserts those just-copied values are `> 0.0` — tautological with respect to
production. The real round-trip *is* exercised elsewhere (`governor.rs` evaluate →
`AgentFitness` → DB), so this is a misleading test name plus redundant coverage,
not a real gap.

**Recommendation:** Either drive the real transfer (`governor.evaluate`, assert
`AgentFitness` fields or a DB round-trip), or rename the test to reflect that it
only checks GPU-slot population.

#### L9. `danger_percept_byte_identical_when_flag_off` only proves flag-off determinism, not byte-identity to a pre-task build
**`tests/integration.rs` (read ≈3932–4054). — NEW.**

The docstring claims flag-off "is byte-identical to a pre-task build (the
byte-identical no-op contract)", but the assertions compare two flag-off runs to
*each other* (`off_phys_a == off_phys_b`, `off_brain_a == off_brain_b`), proving
only run-to-run determinism. A uniform flag-off regression (e.g. always reserving
a danger slot) would produce two identical runs and pass. The layout pins
(`feature_count == base`, sensory stride unchanged) and the off≠on divergence
check give partial protection, but the no-op safety contract is not verified by
byte values here.

**Recommendation:** Pin a golden encoded-state vector captured from the pre-0009
build (as `visual_cortex_passthrough_is_byte_identical` does for 0008) and assert
flag-off matches *it*, not just itself.

#### L10. Generated decision doc omits `num_generations`, world seed, and sample size N; CLI default is 10 generations but the recorded run used 5
**`headless.rs` `format_validation_markdown` (≈975–1100); `main.rs:126` (`default_value_t = 10`). — EXTEND of GPT-5 Codex Note (adds seed + N + structural gap).**

`format_validation_markdown` never emits `num_generations`, the world seed, or
population N; run length is inferable only by counting the 5 trajectory entries,
while the CLI default `validation_generations` is 10. The "canonical baseline"
decision record is thus not self-describing or reproducible. (The runs are
long-lived — mean `ticks_alive` ~999k — so per-agent-tick correlation has sample
support even though genome diversity per generation is small and only one seed was
used.)

**Recommendation:** Emit `num_generations`, world seed, and population N (ideally
per-arm speed variance) into the markdown header.

## Strengths (independently re-verified)

The plan deserves explicit credit — this is a thorough review, not a hit piece:

- **Dual-path parity discipline is excellent.** `split_serial_matches_fused_serial`
  asserts byte-exact equality over the entire 44-slot physics vector, locking all
  five new slots across the fused and split paths, and the seed-42 test world
  genuinely exercises the danger/avoidance slots (not vacuous coverage).
- **Respawn-whitelist correctness.** Every new cumulative accumulator (distance,
  energy, danger-path) is explicitly preserved across death in both paths
  (`phase_death.wgsl`).
- **Rust↔WGSL constant parity for the P_* family is exhaustive and
  self-checking** (`shader_phys_constants_match_rust` plus `PHYS_STRIDE` ==
  max-offset+1 asserts) — the layout cannot silently drift (the WC_*/CFG_* gap in
  N2 is the one asymmetry).
- **Byte-identical no-op at defaults is genuine for encoded state/behavior/fitness.**
  `k=1.0` drag uses `select(...)` to fall back to the exact old expression
  (`speed_cost_exponent_default_is_noop`), `effort_rebased_fitness=false`
  reproduces the old scores, and `danger_percept_enabled=false` leaves
  `FEATURE_COUNT` and encoded brain state unchanged. (The two caveats are L6 and
  L9.)
- **Layered flag gating mirrors the established 0008 `visual_cortex_enabled`
  precedent**, with `#[serde(default)]` back-compat for legacy config blobs.
- **The plan and its decision docs are unusually honest about their own
  deviations.** The calibration doc explicitly records the synthetic-vs-recorded
  deviation and that Layer C does not defeat camping, and STATUS correctly holds
  the default-flip GATED rather than flipping on the questionable validation
  numbers — the right call given the latent miscalibration (H1).
- **The prior reports' top consensus finding is already being addressed.** The
  "pervasive plan references" rule violation flagged by Gemini and Grok is now
  **machine-enforced**: `crates/xagent-sandbox/tests/contributing_guard.rs`
  (added today) ratchets a frozen per-file planning-reference baseline that may
  only shrink, fails `cargo test` on any new/increased reference, and bans
  TODO/FIXME outright; `docs/REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md` covers the judgment-only rules.
  The existing references are grandfathered debt to be scrubbed file-by-file, but
  the leak is now stopped.

## Bottom line

The shipped default build is correct, no-op-safe, well-tested (93/93 green), and
the dual-path engineering is genuinely strong. **Nothing here blocks the current
develop tip.**

But Plan 0009 exists to justify *flipping the effort and danger flags on*, and on
that question the work is not ready. The keystone is **H1**: the effort-rebased
fitness was calibrated against synthetic per-life numbers while production feeds it
cumulative-per-generation accumulators 100–1000× larger, collapsing both effort
axes — the validation doc's own 84% ON-fitness collapse is the evidence the gate
should have read as a failure. The decision machinery that is supposed to catch
this (**M5, M6, L3–L5**) instead overclaims: a gate that passes when the metric
worsens, an uncontrolled A/B, two non-informative conjuncts, and no unit tests.

**Recommended sequence before any default-flip:**

1. Re-derive `FORAGING_ENERGY_TARGET` / `EXPLORATION_DISTANCE_BUDGET` from the
   spec's real recorded-generation replay, or make both axes scale-invariant
   (per-tick rates). Add a production-scale assertion (H1, M1, M2).
2. Add the brain metabolic drain to `P_ENERGY_SPENT` in both shaders, then
   re-calibrate (M3).
3. Fix the avoidance-intent sign inversion and give it a real GPU-level test (M4,
   M7).
4. Harden the gate: strongly-positive-baseline + strict-improvement, seeded paired
   A/B, an uncapped viability metric, a real avoidance threshold in `gate_passed`,
   and unit tests for the correlation/gate math (M5, M6, L3, L4, L5).
5. Sweep the low/nit cleanups (reduction sharing L2, doc/grid/metadata L7/L10,
   the three mis-named tests L8/L9, WC_*/CFG_* parity N2, serde default-off N3,
   `atan2` guard N4, migration ordering N5) and the in-progress plan-reference
   scrub.

Treat the 84% ON mean-fitness collapse as the headline result it is: the current
calibration, if shipped, would make foraging skill nearly invisible to selection.

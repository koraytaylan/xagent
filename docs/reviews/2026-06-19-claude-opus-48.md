## Executive summary

**The engineering is excellent and the measurement culture is unusually honest. The science is not
where the README says it is, and three of the most consequential issues are ones the three other
2026-06-19 reviews did not catch.**

Plan 0010 set out to make 0009's flags safe to graduate by fixing the seven-finding cluster the
2026-06-18 reviews raised. On the **mechanical** items it succeeded cleanly and verifiably:

- **M3 (brain metabolic drain omitted from `P_ENERGY_SPENT`)** — genuinely fixed in *both* shaders
  (`phase_physics.wgsl:181-183`, `kernel_tick.wgsl:231-233`).
- **M4 (avoidance-intent sign inversion)** — fixed *and* backed by a real on-GPU directional test
  (`avoidance_counter_increments_only_on_turn_away`, `integration.rs:6750`) that places danger to the
  right, turns left, and asserts the counter fires in both paths.
- **L6 (danger ring-scan ran unconditionally)**, **N4 (`atan2(0,0)`)**, **N5 (ALTER-before-CREATE)** —
  all correctly fixed.
- **L8 (`recorded_telemetry_persists_in_agent_fitness` was tautological)** — de-tautologized; it now
  runs a real generation and asserts against the production `gov.evaluate()` path.
- **Issue #107, the "brain bifurcation"** that six prior reports (April–June) and *today's Gemini
  review* call the top missing piece — **is resolved in code.** The action policy, value head,
  predictor, and memory all evaluate on the *encoded* state (`brain_passes.wgsl:1268/1281/1106/877`),
  and the encoder is TD-trainable via the credit vector (`:1164` → `:1561`). Gemini's central
  recommendation ("prioritize Issue #107") is **stale**.

But on the **keystone** — the recalibration that the whole plan exists to justify — 0010 did not
deliver, and arguably regressed:

1. **The "Variant B scale-invariant" fitness is not scale-invariant and makes a stationary CAMPER the
   single fittest archetype (HIGH).** The foraging axis is `food·ticks/(energy·250000)`
   (`governor.rs:135-151`) — **`ticks` is in the numerator**, so a long-lived, low-burn camper
   maximizes it. At the synthetic scale the calibration test itself pins camper effort **0.8646** vs
   competent forager **0.3209**; at the decision doc's own production scale the composite is camper
   **0.8594 > competent 0.5292 > aimless 0.1925 > deprived 0.0294**. This is the *exact camping
   inversion* the recalibration was meant to remove, and **no test asserts competent > camper**.
   Independently corroborated three ways: my two skeptics re-ran the test, and the peer
   `2026-06-19-gpt-5-codex.md` (F1/P1) printed the identical numbers. The flag is correctly
   default-off, so blast radius today is zero — but under the project's own *prove-or-kill* discipline
   this is a **measured kill of the headline deliverable**, not a pass.

2. **The default build already violates "no reward functions" (MEDIUM, but the deepest purity breach
   on the floor).** An always-on, **unflagged** potential-based food-approach reward
   (`APPROACH_SHAPING_GAIN=0.05`, `common.wgsl:500`) is folded *unconditionally* into the brain's TD
   reward (`brain_passes.wgsl:802, 823-826`) — note its sibling `danger_shaping` *is* flag-gated
   (`:813`), but `shaping` is not. The README's masthead ("No reward functions… the only evaluative
   signal is homeostatic stability") is contradicted in the shipped default. None of the three peer
   06-19 reviews caught this; two of them explicitly (and incorrectly) state the brain "sees only
   homeostatic deltas." PBRS is policy-invariant so it doesn't move the optimum, and the project's own
   prove-or-kill measured it as a *negative* — so this is a documentation-honesty / drift breach, not
   a behavior bug. But anyone continuing this work must know the default learner optimizes a designer prior.

3. **CI has been silently skipping ~63 of ~95 integration tests, including the entire scientific core
   (HIGH, NEW).** `CLAUDE.md` claims "CI/dev installs Mesa lavapipe," but `ci.yml` has *no* such
   step — it runs `cargo test -p xagent-sandbox` on bare `ubuntu-latest`, where `GpuKernel::is_available()`
   returns false, so 63 GPU-gated tests `eprintln!("Skipping…"); return;` and pass green without
   executing the kernel. The falsifiable steering probe, every byte-identical no-op parity proof, and
   every fused/split determinism test are among them. "Green on CI" is not evidence of GPU-path
   correctness, and no run-count gate exists to catch an all-skip. (`release.yml` has the same gap.)

And on the founding question — **does homeostasis-driven predictive processing produce emergent
vision-conditional cognition?** — the answer at `HEAD` is still **no, and the project says so honestly.**
`learning_probe_mirrored_steering_is_chance` is *green precisely because* steering sits at chance
(0.474, verified live) after 120 mirrored training episodes, even though the food side is linearly
separable in the encoded state (~55× margin) — i.e. the encoder is **not** the bottleneck; the
credit/temporal path is.

**Verdict.** Mechanically healthy, ship-ready at defaults, no blockers on the tip. **Maintenance
burden: HIGH but bounded** (dual Rust+WGSL expertise, byte-identical parity discipline, a 4,990-line
governor, a real bus-factor on the credit-assignment layer). **Drift from goals: real and asymmetric** —
the lifetime learner already carries an undocumented handcrafted reward (a genuine breach), while the
more-visible intent-aware fitness and visual priors are flag-off no-ops (latent drift). **Closeness to
the core hypothesis: LOW, and honestly measured.** The recent 21-task hardening plan is careful,
honest work that fixed the lower-severity items but did not (could not) certify the keystone — and the
gate that was supposed to certify it is itself unsound.

### The keystone, falsified (HIGH — REC1)

The recalibration replaced 0009's collapsing axes with a "per-tick-rate scale-invariant Variant B."
The code comment claims the axis is "independent of survival length" (`governor.rs:114-120`), and the
test is titled accordingly. **It is mathematically false.** `foraging = (food/(energy/ticks))/TARGET =
food·ticks/(energy·250000)`. Because food and energy both accumulate with lifespan, the `ticks`
numerator means a longer life inflates the ratio; a stationary camper (huge `ticks`, tiny `energy`)
saturates it at 1.0. Empirically (test fixtures `governor.rs:2410-2475`, run live by two skeptics and
independently by `2026-06-19-gpt-5-codex.md`):

```
Synthetic 100k-tick:   Camper effort 0.8646  >  Competent 0.3209  >  Fast-aimless 0.1343
Production 1M-tick:    Camper 0.8594  >  Competent 0.5292  >  Aimless 0.1925  >  Deprived 0.0294
```

The camper — the behavior the effort-rebasing was explicitly invented to defeat ("food-per-energy to
defeat camping") — is the single fittest archetype at both scales. **No assertion guards
`competent > camper`** (`fitness_calibration_replay_profiles` asserts only `competent > aimless` and
`camper < 1.0`, and even *pins* `camper ≈ 0.8646` as expected — `governor.rs:3093-3135`). The
"calibrated from real production-scale telemetry" claim is also unsubstantiated: the decision doc's own
*Telemetry Source* section concedes `load_recording` does **not** store the cumulative accumulators
needed, and the archetype numbers are hand-authored constants, not measured replay (REC2, LOW, AGREE
with prior M1). **Recommendation:** do not graduate `effort_rebased_fitness`. Remove `ticks` from the
foraging numerator (denominate on total energy) or add an explicit within-life-improvement /
minimum-movement term, and add a `competent > camper` production-scale assertion before any flip.

### Residual gate/harness weaknesses (LOW — AGREE with the 2026-06-18 set and 06-19 peers)

- **REC3 / gate signed-semantics (AGREE gpt-5-codex F3):** the M5 gate criterion text says "falls from
  strongly-*positive*," but the predicate uses `baseline_corr.abs() >= MIN`, so a strongly-*negative*
  baseline also passes. Tighten to a signed lower bound.
- **REC4 / partial pairing (AGREE gpt-5-codex F2, opus-0618 M6):** the seeded paired A/B is fixed only
  at generation 0; multi-generation breeding still draws unseeded `rand::rng()`, so the multi-gen delta
  remains noisy. `seeded_ab_arms_are_paired` proves only helper-level determinism (AB1).
- **REC7 / REC10 (mixed):** `nearest_danger_bearing_points_at_danger` now asserts the bearing is
  finite and in `[-π,π]` but still **not** that it points *at* the danger (a residual of prior M7); and
  `fitness_calibration_replay_profiles` ships an internally-stale explanatory comment
  (`governor.rs:3078` claims `exploration=0.6` / foraging `1.0` for the competent profile whose pinned
  value is 0.3209).

## Part 2 — Findings by severity

Severity uses the **post-verification** level (a skeptic could downgrade or drop). Each is tagged
**NEW / AGREE / EXTEND / CONTRADICT** vs prior reports.

### HIGH

**H1 — Core hypothesis is falsified at `HEAD`: vision-conditional steering is at chance.** *(learning;
AGREE `2026-06-14-claude-opus-48.md` F1/F2, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-06-10-learning-baseline.md`;
`integration.rs:2719-2794`, `:2812-2890`.)* Run live on a real adapter: mirrored-steering alignment
290/612 = **0.474** (chance band `[0.38,0.62]`, GREEN), baseline 0.497, encoder food-side separability
within-side 0.0002 vs between-side 0.0111 (**~55× margin**). After 120 mirrored episodes at the densest
single-tick transitions the agent does not steer toward seen food, even though the side is strongly
linearly represented. This is the project's own deliberately-pinned baseline (a *strength* of honesty),
so it is the central **closeness-to-goals characterization**, not a code defect — but it is the No. 1
gap between the codebase and its founding claim. Treat this probe as the load-bearing acceptance gate.

**H2 — Credit-isolation root cause: the turn channel cannot be credited.** *(learning; AGREE
`2026-06-14-claude-opus-48.md`, the learning-baseline spec; `brain_passes.wgsl:1129-1164, 1499-1502,
791-826`; `common.wgsl:529-533`; `buffers.rs:737,780`.)* The TD reward `raw_gradient =
energy_delta·0.6 + integrity_delta·0.4 + shaping` is dominated within a brain tick by *forward*
displacement; a turn only changes bearing, whose benefit accrues over many later forward steps. Both
forward and turn eligibility traces are `noise·s_encoded` decaying at `γλ ≈ 0.873` (~8-tick horizon),
and the same action-blind `td_error` multiplies both — so `E[td_error·noise_turn·enc] ≈ 0` and the turn
weights (zero-init; only `INITIAL_FORWARD_BIAS=0.3` is nonzero) never accumulate sign-correct credit.
This is the verified mechanism behind H1 and matches the project's own recorded diagnosis. **The fix is
known and architectural:** an action-conditioned advantage or an explicit bearing-aligned turn-credit
term, not more encoder work.

**H3 — Variant B keystone falsified; camper is fittest.** *(recent-change; AGREE `2026-06-19-gpt-5-codex.md`
F1/P1, EXTEND opus-0618 H1 to production scale; CONTRADICT `2026-06-19-gemini-31-pro-high.md`'s claim
that 0010/0011 "resolved" the fitness finding.)* See Part 1. Graduation-blocking; default-off so live
impact is zero today.

**H4 — CI silently skips ~63 of ~95 integration tests (NEW).** *(testing/CI; `.github/workflows/ci.yml`,
`gpu_kernel.rs:466-484`, `CLAUDE.md`.)* No Mesa/lavapipe/apt step in `ci.yml` (or `release.yml`), so on
`ubuntu-latest` the 63 GPU-gated tests self-skip green — including `learning_probe_mirrored_steering_is_chance`,
every `*_byte_identical_when_flag_off` no-op proof, every determinism/parity test, and the 0010 hardening
guards (`energy_spent_includes_brain_drain`, `seeded_ab_arms_are_paired`, the avoidance sign test). Only
~32 CPU-only tests run in CI. This silently nullifies regression coverage for ~65% of the suite and
makes "green on CI" meaningless for the GPU core (it *does* run on the author's local machine
pre-commit, which is why this is a coverage/doc gap, not a correctness bug). **Fix:** install lavapipe +
`VK_ICD_FILENAMES`/`LIBGL_ALWAYS_SOFTWARE` in CI (as `CLAUDE.md` implies was intended), or at minimum
fail CI when the GPU-test run count is zero. No prior report raised this.

### MEDIUM

**M1 — Always-on, unflagged approach-shaping reward in the default brain.** *(drift; EXTEND
`2026-06-19-gemini-31-pro-high.md`, NEW vs all three 06-19 peers which missed it; CONTRADICT grok-43's
"brain sees only homeostatic deltas"; `brain_passes.wgsl:802,823-826`, `common.wgsl:494-500`.)* See
Executive summary §2. Real docs-vs-code breach in both READMEs; policy-invariant and measured-inert, so
MEDIUM. Either gate it default-off like every sibling directional signal, or document it explicitly.

**M2 — Intent-aware fitness is a handcrafted selection reward (latent drift).** *(drift; AGREE
`2026-06-19-gemini-31-pro-high.md`; `governor.rs:135-151,699`, `config.rs:465-467,555-557,587-590`.)*
`food-per-energy` / `cells-per-distance` axes encode a designer's notion of "good." Correctly fenced:
default-false in all three presets, `#[serde(default)]` back-compat, non-heritable, byte-identical
no-op, and avoidance-intent is observability-only (never a selection term). This is opt-in latent

**M3 — Recording table is an unbounded-growth disk liability (NEW).** *(governor; `evolution.rs:235-239`,
`governor.rs:1398-1489,1826-1833`.)* Every generation's full per-tick BLOB (`agent_count·ticks·15`
floats) is persisted forever — no `record_every_n`, no `max_retained`, no opt-out, no DELETE/prune, no
`ON DELETE CASCADE`. Measured: `generation_recording` is ~1.62 GB of the 1.6 GB local `xagent.db` (626

headless batch runner never records), so this is an operational/ops concern, not repo bloat or a
selection bug. Add a retention knob or champion-lineage-only retention.

**M4 — Terminal-death TD update diverges 8× between fused and split paths (EXTEND; CONTRADICT grok-43's
"fused/split exact symmetry on every accumulator").** *(GPU; `kernel_tick.wgsl:642-643` vs
`phase_death.wgsl:147-148`; `common.wgsl:541,548`.)* The fused death path scales the forward/turn
**action-weight** terminal lesson by `ACTOR_VECTOR_SCALE` (1/16), matching the within-life learner; the
split `phase_death.wgsl` uses `TD_VECTOR_SCALE` (1/128) — an 8× weaker lesson (critic weights use 1/128
in both, so the bug is isolated to the actor). I verified this by direct inspection. The fused path was
migrated to `ACTOR_VECTOR_SCALE` in `9f80cea0`; `phase_death.wgsl` was never updated. It is **live in
the default runtime**: `dispatch_ticks_fused_serial` runs the split `physics_pipeline` for the
physics-only remainder whenever `ticks_to_run % brain_tick_stride != 0`, which the wall-clock-driven
interactive loop hits frequently (`sim_runtime.rs:563`) — so an agent's terminal lesson depends on
*which dispatch processed its death*. No parity test catches it because every parity test deliberately
avoids killing an agent. The headless **scoring** path is clean (its 100-tick cadence is a stride
multiple, remainder = 0), which is why this is MEDIUM not HIGH. Fix `phase_death.wgsl` to use
`ACTOR_VECTOR_SCALE` and add a death-crossing parity test.

**M5 — Calibration test pins camper > forager but never asserts the forager wins.** *(testing; AGREE
opus-0618 H1, gpt-5-codex F1; `governor.rs:3093-3143`.)* The test-completeness twin of H3: it pins
`camper 0.8646` and `competent 0.3209`, asserts `competent > aimless`, but never `competent > camper`,
so the proxy-gaming inversion is *encoded as expected behavior*. Two adjacent comments overclaim
("`prevents camping`" `:56`; "camping-proof" `:2310`).

## Part 3 — Drift from original goals

The README sells a purist vision: *"No hardcoded goals. No reward functions… the only evaluative signal
is homeostatic stability… organisms don't optimize for reward."* Against the six load-bearing pillars:

| Pillar | Drift | Reality at `HEAD` |
|---|---|---|
| **No reward functions / homeostasis-only** | **MAJOR** | Violated in the *default* path: an always-on, unflagged approach-shaping reward is in the TD signal (M1). The flag-off intent-aware fitness (M2) is a second, latent instance in the selection loop. |
| **Numerically flattened, opaque interface** | minor | Substantially intact and *honestly* caveated (README §2 concedes the fixed modality layout / touch priors / `surface_tag` channel). Hubel-Wiesel priors are dormant (L14). |
| **Emergent anti-loop / curiosity / habit** | minor | Present and heritable (habituation EMA, curiosity bonus, motor fatigue, klinotaxis) — partly hand-built scaffolding, which the README's opening line admits. |
| **Evolution selects on emergent fitness** | minor | Generational selection works (statistically literate: pooled-SE significance gate, mean-not-max to fight winner's curse, islands, momentum mutation). ## Bottom line

The shipped default is correct, no-op-safe, and green (217 tests). The fused-kernel engine, the
worker boundary, the reunified brain, and the falsifiable-measurement culture are genuinely strong, and
Plan 0010 cleaned up the lower-severity 0009 findings with real, well-tested fixes — including resolving
the Issue #107 bifurcation that six prior reports and today's Gemini review still treat as the top open
problem.

But three things must be internalized as the project moves forward, none of which the three concurrent 2026-06-19
reviews caught: **(1)** the keystone of the most recent 21-task plan — the "scale-invariant" effort
fitness — is mathematically not scale-invariant and makes a stationary camper the single fittest
agent, so it cannot be graduated and the test that should guard it instead pins the inversion as
expected; **(2)** the *default* build already injects an always-on, undocumented handcrafted
food-approach reward into the brain's TD signal, contradicting the README's flagship "no reward
functions" claim — and steering *still* fails at chance despite that crutch; **(3)** CI has been
silently skipping ~two-thirds of the test suite, so "green on CI" is not evidence of GPU-path
correctness.

The honest reading looking ahead: **the engineering is excellent and the science is rigorously measured,
but the core hypothesis remains unproven-to-failing, the documentation overstates both throughput and
philosophical purity, and the effort to sustain it is high (dual Rust+WGSL, parity discipline, a 4,990-line
governor, a real bus factor on the credit layer).** This is a serious, well-run research codebase whose
next phase hinges on either proving the credit path or honestly reframing the goal; sustaining it will
remain a high-touch effort, and progressing it demands the same dual-domain depth that built it.
